About

I am a first-year research master's student in Tunisia with a background in software engineering and information technology, and I plan to pursue a PhD.

My interests span artificial intelligence, machine learning, large language models, AI safety and security, efficiency, multi-agent systems, responsible AI and optimisation. I am especially interested in how these fields can support data-driven decision-making.

For my final-year project, I built a multilingual retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) research assistant. It combined document ingestion, semantic search with citations, and a chat interface, using FastAPI and PostgreSQL with vector search.
